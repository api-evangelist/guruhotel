# GuruHotel

> GuruHotel is AI-native direct booking infrastructure for independent hotels: an AI-powered hotel website builder, direct booking engine and payment orchestration platform that turns a hotel's official website into a direct booking channel with real-time inventory, secure Stripe-powered payments and a guest-first checkout.

GuruHotel is trusted by 1000+ hotels, serves 200,000+ guests per year and processes $15M+ in annual gross booking value across the US, Mexico, the Caribbean and Europe. It has used Stripe as its payment partner since 2019 (Checkout, Elements, Payment Links, Link, Apple Pay and Google Pay). Multi-language booking and multi-currency checkout are supported.
